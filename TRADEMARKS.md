## What MIT lets you do

You may use, copy, modify, merge, publish, distribute, sublicense and sell
copies of the **code**, provided you keep the copyright and permission notice.

## What it does not let you do

Without written permission from WinterGate you may not:

- name a fork or derivative **Ominis-OSINT**, **Ominis** or **Secure Web-Hunter**,
  or use a name or logo that is confusingly similar;
- use the marks in a way that suggests your build is official, endorsed or
  supported by WinterGate;
- redistribute the UI assets as your own product branding.

If you publish a modified build, give it your own name and make it clear that it
is a fork. "Based on Ominis-OSINT (MIT)" is fine; "Ominis-OSINT Pro" is not.
