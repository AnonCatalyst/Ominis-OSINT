# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Ominis-OSINT to be a place where anyone can report a bug, suggest a
feature or send a patch without being harassed. We pledge to make participation
welcoming regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance,
race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that helps:

- Being kind and precise in reviews.
- Assuming good faith while asking for evidence.
- Accepting that a maintainer may decline a feature that widens the attack
  surface, adds a mandatory dependency or turns on proxies by default.
- Respecting privacy: never post someone else's personal data, credentials or
  private logs.

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- Harassment, insults, sexualised language or unwelcome attention.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Encouraging unlawful access, credential theft or targeting of individuals.
- Sustained disruption of issues, pull requests or discussions.

## Scope

This code applies in the repository, its issues and pull requests, and in any
space where someone represents the project.

## Enforcement

Report incidents to <conduct@wintergate.org>. Include links or screenshots and
what outcome you would like. Reports are handled privately by the maintainers.

Consequences are proportionate: a private warning, a temporary ban from
interaction, or a permanent ban. Maintainers who violate this code face the same
process, handled by another maintainer where possible.
